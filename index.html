Our Services

Interior and exterior work, handled end to end.

Kitchen Remodeling

Cabinetry, countertops, tile, lighting and finish carpentry — planned around how the room is actually used, and detailed so the finished surfaces line up.

Bathroom Remodeling

Full rebuilds and focused updates: waterproofing and tile, vanities, fixtures, ventilation and finishes that stand up to daily use.

Interior Renovation

Single rooms through whole-home renovations — framing, drywall, doors, trim and finish work, sequenced so the job keeps moving.

Exterior Renovation

Siding, trim, decks, fences and outdoor spaces — built and sealed to handle weather season after season.

Flooring

Hardwood, laminate, tile and vinyl, including subfloor prep, level transitions and trim details.

Painting

Interior and exterior painting with the prep that makes it last — patching, sanding, priming and clean, straight edges.

Repairs & Improvements

Smaller jobs handled properly: drywall repair, doors and trim, and the targeted improvements that keep a house in good shape.
